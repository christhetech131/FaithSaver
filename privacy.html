FaithSaver — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-10-30

Summary

The FaithSaver screensaver itself does not include analytics or tracking. It loads an offline first frame and then fetches images from a public GitHub repository for display. The project website is a static GitHub Pages site. If you submit content, we receive your submission and any contact details you provide.

What the Roku screensaver does

  • Network requests: The saver makes requests to GitHub’s API and content servers to retrieve image lists and image files for your selected category.
  • No telemetry: The saver does not send analytics events, advertising identifiers, or other tracking data to us.
  • Device data: We don’t receive device identifiers or IP addresses from Roku devices. When the saver fetches images from GitHub, GitHub may receive device IP and user‑agent information in the ordinary course of delivering content, governed by their policies.

Website (GitHub Pages)

  • Our site doesn’t set cookies or run third‑party analytics scripts.
  • GitHub Pages and related hosting may maintain server logs (e.g., IP address, user‑agent) as part of operating the service. Those logs are controlled by GitHub.

Submissions (Dropbox and email)

  • Dropbox File Requests: If you upload an image via the provided Dropbox links, Dropbox receives the file and associated metadata to deliver it to us. Your use of Dropbox is subject to Dropbox’s terms and privacy policy.
  • Email: If you email a verse or other content, we’ll receive your email address and any information you include. We use this to review, curate, and attribute submissions.
  • Attribution: When you provide a name for credit, we may publish that name in the repository or on images, consistent with the submission’s license.

How we use and share information

  • We use submissions to curate and publish images for the screensaver.
  • Approved images may be stored publicly in the GitHub repository and displayed in the screensaver.
  • We don’t sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Data retention

Approved submissions may be retained indefinitely in the public repository. Unapproved submissions may be retained for review for a limited period. You can request removal of your content by emailing us; we’ll make reasonable efforts to comply when feasible and lawful.

Children’s privacy

FaithSaver isn’t directed to children and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us and we’ll address it.

Your choices

  • You can uninstall the screensaver at any time via your Roku device.
  • You can choose not to submit images or verses.
  • You can email us to request updates or removal of your submission or attribution.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we’ll update the effective date above. Your continued use means you accept the updated policy.
